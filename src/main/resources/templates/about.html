Who We Are

Bienvenue ! Nous sommes Mon Petit Café. Established in 2016, we strive to serve you only the best from bean to cup. Our beans are single-origin, sourced 100% ethically, and roasted in-house for the freshest, most authentic experience. Our syrups are also homemade, and our milk is produced locally from a dairy farm just outside of town.

Don't drink milk? No problem! We also provide milk alternatives at no additional cost to you.
